We do not choose sides. We choose tobacco that earns its place.

Havana brings history; Nicaragua brings muscle; the Dominican Republic brings polish; Honduras brings earth. Our job is to place them honestly next to each other — then let your palate decide. No franchise script, no single-flag loyalty.
